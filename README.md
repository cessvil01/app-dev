## My Favorite Series: Ginny & Georgia 

**Ginny & Georgia** is an American coming-of-age comedy drama television series created by **Sarah Lampert**.

> *"We're the new Millers... and we're not to be messed with. "* - Georgia Miller

### Key Details:
* **Genre:** Coming-of-age, Comedy-drama, Mystery
* **Creator:** Sarah Lampert
* **Starring:** Antonia Gentry (as Ginny Miller), Brianne Howey (as Georgia Miller)
* **Seasons:** 3 (as of late 2025)
* **Status:** Renewed for Season 4!!!
---
The show centers around the complicated lives of mother and daughter, **Georgia** and **Ginny Miller**, as they try to settle down in a New England town after years on the run. **Ginny** deals with teenage angstand identity, while **Georgia's** mysterious past threatens their new, seemingly perfect life.

I love the series because of its:
1. **Complex Characters:** The depth and flaws of the main characters make them incredibly compelling.
2. **Fast-paced Plot:** There is always a new secret or twist, keeping you hooked.
3. **Exploration of Themes:** It tackles important issues like race, mental health and class.

***
*The perfect blend of drama, humor, and heart.*
